Scope

What the diagnostic examines

First impression

Visitors form a judgement of a company within seconds. Layout, typography, imagery and hierarchy must match the standing of the brand.

Mobile experience

Most web traffic worldwide comes from phones. Navigation, text size and behaviour on a small screen are reviewed page by page.

Advertising landing pages

Paid traffic is only as effective as the page it lands on. Message match, clarity of the offer and the path to contact are assessed.

Languages and markets

Content in the visitor’s own language, structured correctly for search, carries a brand into new markets.

Contact path

Every page needs one clear next step. Forms, calls to action and the response flow are examined.

Measurement

Without correct tracking, advertising spend cannot be evaluated. Analytics and conversion set-up are verified.
